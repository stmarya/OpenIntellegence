# AI Analyst & Intelligence Production

## Supported outcomes

- Grounded analyst chat
- Cited correlation briefs
- AI-assisted intelligence reports

## Grounding policy

The service retrieves relevant platform records before generating factual claims. Citations are stored alongside generated output. When no supporting evidence is retrieved, output is explicitly `unverified` or withheld.

## Prohibited behavior

The AI layer must not invent evidence, represent uncertain attribution as fact, expose cross-tenant records, approve automation, or execute commands.
