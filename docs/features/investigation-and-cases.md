# Investigation & Case Management

## Outcome

Analysts can collect entities, evidence, tasks, ownership, SLA context, and an append-only activity timeline into an auditable case workspace.

## Domain rules

- Investigations and cases are tenant-scoped.
- Evidence links preserve source/provenance context.
- Timeline events append; they are not silently rewritten.
- Automation may propose case creation but cannot create or close high-impact work without authorized approval.
